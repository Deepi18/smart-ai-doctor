About Smart AI Doctor & Technology

Smart AI Doctor is an advanced clinical decision support platform combining machine learning disease classification, Natural Language Processing, and LLM medical reasoning.

Machine Learning Classifier

Trained on multi-symptom vectors covering 30+ acute and chronic medical conditions. Uses Scikit-Learn Random Forests to calculate probability scores and triage urgency.

NLP Keyword Parser

Extracts clinical symptom keywords directly from free-form natural language patient descriptions, normalizing synonyms into standard medical vocabulary.

ReportLab Engine

Generates downloadable PDF Medical Assessment Summary reports featuring patient demographics, diagnostic probability bars, precautions, and specialist advice.

Medical Disclaimer & Data Privacy

Smart AI Doctor is designed strictly as an artificial intelligence educational and decision-support tool. It is NOT a substitute for professional clinical medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified physician or healthcare provider for medical concerns. In an emergency, contact emergency medical services immediately.
